Hello,

This is an educational phishing-awareness template. In real life, messages like this may try to create urgency and push you to click a link.

Common warning signs

  • Urgent language (“verify now”, “account will be locked”)
  • Suspicious sender address
  • Links that do not match the real domain
  • Requests for passwords or codes

Safe behavior: Do not click suspicious links. Verify through official websites/apps.

Educational content only — no real credentials should ever be entered.
